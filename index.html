Lane Legends

Push the lane with your minions, break two towers, and destroy the red base. Kill the enemy hero to slow their push.

Move: joystick or WASD. Attack: ⚔️ or J (hold). Blast: 💥 or K. Surge heal + speed: ✨ or L. Ultimate: 🔥 or I. Drag a skill button to aim it and release to cast. A quick tap uses auto-aim.

AurelionBlade knight. Fast melee combos with lunges and knockback. Every 4th hit is a critical. 💥 Blast, ✨ Surge heal.
VoltraStorm mage. Ranged arcane bolts. 💥 Chain lightning jumps between 4 enemies, ✨ Blink dashes forward.
KestrelMarksman archer. Long-range arrows. 💥 Piercing Shot hits a whole line, ✨ Roll dodges back, 🔥 Arrow Rain.
